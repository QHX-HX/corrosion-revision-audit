# Response letter skeleton

Replace bracketed text. Keep one comment block per reviewer item, in the reviewer's order.

```text
Response to Reviewers

Manuscript number: [ID]
Title: [title]
Journal: [journal]

General Response

Dear Editor and Reviewers,

We thank the editor and the reviewers for their careful reading of our manuscript
and their constructive comments. We have revised the manuscript thoroughly. Major
changes include:

1. [numerical corrections]
2. [additional experiments or analyses]
3. [revision of mechanistic interpretation]
4. [methodological and statistical supplements]
5. [editorial corrections]

Point-by-point responses follow.

Reviewer 1 (Major revision)

R1-1  [short title]

Comment: [verbatim reviewer text]

Response: [what was done, why, and where; one paragraph, <= 180 words]

Changes made: [section/page/table/figure list]

R1-2  [short title]

Comment: ...

Response: ...

Changes made: ...
```

## Response paragraph pattern

1. Acknowledge the specific point in one clause (no formulaic thanks).
2. State what was changed and the new value/claim.
3. State where it appears.
4. If you disagree, give the evidence first, then the compromise.

## Anti-patterns

- "We agree" when nothing changed.
- Describing a change that is not in the manuscript.
- Repeating the reviewer's criticism without a resolution.
- Internal vocabulary: route A/B, red line, TODO, planning tables, Chinese notes.
- Sentences longer than ~40 words; they read as defensive.
